What's in the Set

The Costume Creation Repertoire

01

Custom Commissions

A costume built from scratch to a brief: your concept, your measurements, your palette. Designed, patterned and sewn in-house from first sketch to final fitting.

02

Character Builds

Full-body character costuming — creature, alien, animal and story looks, including the prosthetic and body-paint styling that completes them.

03

Wings & Large Wearables

Wings, oversized silhouettes and structural pieces built to be worn and moved in, not just photographed standing still.

04

Crowns, Headpieces & Accessories

Crowns, headdresses, harnesses and detail pieces — the parts that carry a look in close-up and on camera.

05

Stilt Costumes

Costuming built for ten-foot proportions, where an ordinary garment simply reads wrong.

06

LED & Illuminated Costumes

Wearables built around programmable light, so the costume itself is part of the show after the house lights drop.

07

Fire-Safe Wearables

The fire skirt is the clearest example — burning garments built with the material choices and safety margins that make them performable rather than decorative.

08

Performance Wardrobe

Costuming for aerial, pole-free floor work, flow and dance, cut so it survives the apparatus and holds its shape upside down.

09

Brand & Theme Builds

Costumes made in a brand's exact palette or to an event's theme, for a cast rather than a single performer.

Where & How It Happens

Booking Costume Creation

For performers

Working aerialists, fire performers, dancers and flow artists who need wardrobe that survives an apparatus and still photographs well.

For producers & casts

Matched wardrobe across a cast, built to one design language.

For brands & activations

Costuming in a brand's palette, built to a campaign concept — usually commissioned alongside a performance booking.

For private clients

Festival wear, Halloween builds, themed party looks and one-off statement pieces.

Remote clients

Commissions ship nationwide. Fit is worked from a measurement set with a video fitting before the final make.
