For Costumers

Fit Notes

Fit tip
Very broad back and shoulders (V-taper). Fitted or non-stretch tops and dresses should be sized to the 37″ bust and 18″ shoulders, then taken in at the waist.
Loves
LED costumes.
Head pieces
Heavy head pieces can cause tension headaches — lighter pieces work best.
Arms-up looks
Please give a heads-up if a costume requires the arms to stay up the entire time.

Stilts

Drywall stilts
Surpro double-walled drywall stilts with a spring under the toe
Peg stilts
Voltege 3 ft pegs
