What the venue needs to provide

Figures tagged Caroline to confirm are drafted from standard industry practice and still need to be checked against her actual equipment before this page goes to a client.

Pole
Venue-supplied house pole preferred. A portable pole can be arranged — confirm early, as it changes the fee and the load-in Caroline to confirm
Ceiling height
About 10–14 ft for a standard stage pole Caroline to confirm
Floor & ceiling load
A stage pole needs a solid, level floor and a structural ceiling if it is a pressure-mount — a freestanding base is used otherwise Caroline to confirm
Clear area
Roughly 10 ft × 10 ft around the pole Caroline to confirm
Pole condition
Clean, dry and degreased before the set. No lotion, glitter or drink spills on the pole
Costuming
Styled to the room — glamorous, burlesque, or covered. Please state the audience and dress code when booking
Set structure
Featured sets of 4–6 minutes, or ambient sets of 20 minutes Caroline to confirm
