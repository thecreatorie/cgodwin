What's in the Set

The Hoop & Flow Repertoire

01

LED Hoops — up to four

Programmable Pyroterra hoops in the client's colours. Multi-hoop isolations, splits and body rolls.

02

Fire Hoops — up to two

One or two burning hoops at once. Performed at Las Vegas PRIDE.

03

Rhinestone Quad Hoops

Four crystal-covered hoops that catch venue lighting instead of generating their own — the black-tie option that needs no darkness and no flame.

04

Gyroscopic Hoop

A single hoop worked through gyroscopic patterns for a slower, more hypnotic feel.

05

Mini Double Hoops

Fast, tight hand-hooping for close crowds and camera work.

06

Rope Dart & Juggling Clubs

Additional flow props — LED rope dart, and club balance for roaming and photo moments.

07

Doubles & Group

Hoop duets, and larger flow casts bookable through her entertainment company.

Where & How It Happens

Booking Hoop & Flow

Anywhere with floor space

No rigging, no ceiling requirement beyond arm's reach — hooping is the act that fits venues nothing else fits.

Nightclubs & festivals

LED hoops in the dark, roaming or on a podium.

Corporate & gala

Rhinestone hoops under existing venue lighting, no flame and no blackout required.

Pride, parades & street

Fire hoops outdoors with a safety, or LED after dark.

Workshops

Hoop classes for festivals and private groups — see Workshops & Coaching.

Content shoots

Long-exposure LED hooping for stills and reels.

What the venue needs to provide

Figures tagged Caroline to confirm are drafted from standard industry practice and still need to be checked against her actual equipment before this page goes to a client.

Performance area
Roughly 10 ft × 10 ft clear for single hoop; about 15 ft × 15 ft for multi-hoop or fire Caroline to confirm
Overhead clearance
About 10 ft; hoops go overhead constantly Caroline to confirm
Lighting
LED needs the room dark. Rhinestone hoops need the opposite — bright, directional light
Power
One 110 V outlet for charging between sets
Fire hoops
Outdoors or venue-approved only, with the same fire conditions listed on the Fire page
Set structure
Roaming in 20–30 minute sets, or featured sets of 4–6 minutes Caroline to confirm
Flooring
Any flat floor. Hoops drop — a surface that will not be damaged by a dropped hoop is preferable
