Short bio

Caroline Godwin is a Las Vegas circus performer, fire dancer, aerialist and burlesque artist, known for the Fire Aerial Pentagram. Booked at EDC, Formula 1, NASCAR, Burning Man and Las Vegas PRIDE, she builds, choreographs and costumes every act herself.

Full bio

Caroline began her journey in the circus arts with prop manipulation in 2014 and now has over a decade of experience in flow arts. She started aerial in 2018, moved to Las Vegas to take her career to the next level, and became a professional circus artist in 2020.

She has performed at some of the largest events in Nevada — EDC, Formula 1, NASCAR, Burning Man, Las Vegas PRIDE, the Demolition of the Historic Tropicana and the Grand Reopening of the Palms — plus Dreamforce, AWS and the Zouk, TAO and Terrace Afterhours nightclubs, sharing stages with Marshmello, Tiësto, RÜFÜS DU SOL, Chris Lake and Josh Teed.

Her signature act is the Fire Aerial Pentagram. She is also a known burlesque artist, featured alongside Raquel Reed, Cervena Fox and Fawnia Mondey in TASTE, Odyssey Noir, Fetish & Fantasy, Tinsel and Jiggleroom. She designs and sews her own costumes, builds custom fire props, and programs her own LED shows for corporate and themed events.

Caroline is a sponsored performer for Juggling Calling and Pyrotechnique Las Vegas, teaches weekly at Pole Fitness Studio in Las Vegas, and teaches fire aerial classes at festivals including Flowstorm and Southwest Flow Festival.
