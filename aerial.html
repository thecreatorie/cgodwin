What's in the Set

The Aerial Repertoire

01

The Fire Aerial Pentagram

Her signature and, as far as she knows, the only one of its kind — a five-point steel pentagram that flies with fire wicks burning at every vertex. Available small or large, with fire, with LED, or clean. This is the act people book her for.

02

Lyra / Aerial Hoop

The workhorse. Single point or double point, static or spinning, in a plain steel hoop, a full programmable LED hoop, or rhinestoned for a black-tie room.

03

Aerial Sphere

A caged globe that reads beautifully from every angle — built for centre-of-room installs and 360° audiences. Performed at AREA15 in full alien character.

04

Lollipop Lyra

A hoop on a rigid pole standing on its own base. No rigging, no ceiling, no ground crew — it lands anywhere there's a flat floor, and the stage itself is available to rent.

05

Aerial Hammock & Sling

Softer, faster, more theatrical. Good for tight ceilings and for projection-mapped rooms.

06

Aerial Heart

A heart-shaped apparatus — the obvious call for weddings, Valentine's events and romance-styled brand work.

07

Chains, Loops & Dance Trapeze

Harder-edged apparatuses for darker rooms — chains in particular photograph brutally well under red light.

08

Aerial Fire & Fire Breathing

Fire wicks mounted to the apparatus, or fire breathing performed from the air. The most requested upgrade, and the one with the most technical conditions attached.

Where & How It Happens

Booking Aerial

Venues with rigging points

Theatres, ballrooms, nightclubs and arenas with certified points. Caroline works with the house rigger and supplies her own hardware and inspection paperwork.

Venues with no rigging at all

Her self-standing rig travels. It has flown at Burning Man on open playa, in parking lots, on grass, on concrete and on the Esplanade — anywhere a truck can reach and the ground is level.

Outdoors & festivals

Desert, beach, racetrack, festival field. Wind is the limiting factor, not the surface.

Stages & art cars

She has performed on the Maxa art car above the Burning Man crowd during RÜFÜS DU SOL, and on standard proscenium and thrust stages.

Ceremonies

Weddings — including a wiccan handfasting on the pentagram — vow renewals, memorials and brand launches, where the act is choreographed to a specific moment rather than a set.

What the venue needs to provide

Figures tagged Caroline to confirm are drafted from standard industry practice and still need to be checked against her actual equipment before this page goes to a client.

Ceiling height
Roughly 18 ft minimum for a full lyra or pentagram act; short-ceiling versions can be choreographed down to about 14 ft Caroline to confirm
Rigging point
One certified point rated to the load below, or her self-standing rig instead
Dynamic load rating
1,000 lb / 4.5 kN minimum per point for a single-performer dynamic act Caroline to confirm
Self-standing rig footprint
About 16 ft × 16 ft of level ground plus clearance, on a surface that will take the base plates Caroline to confirm
Rig setup time
2–3 hours in, 1–2 hours out, with 1–2 crew for assist Caroline to confirm
Performance space
A clear circle roughly 15 ft across beneath the apparatus, kept free of guests and furniture Caroline to confirm
Power
One standard 110 V circuit for LED charging and haze; none required for a clean act
Insurance
Licensed and insured; certificate of insurance provided to the venue on request
Set structure
Typical booking is 3 × 6–8 minute sets across an evening, or one featured 5–7 minute act Caroline to confirm
