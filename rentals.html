What's in the Set

The Rentals Repertoire

01

Self-Standing Aerial Rig

A freestanding rig that puts a certified aerial point anywhere a truck can reach — it has flown on open playa, in parking lots, on grass and on concrete. No ceiling required.

02

Lollipop Lyra Stage

A hoop on a rigid pole standing on its own base. No rigging, no ground crew, and it lands on any flat floor — the most-hired single piece in the inventory.

03

Lyra & Aerial Hoop

Single and double point, plain steel, full programmable LED, or rhinestoned for a black-tie room.

04

Aerial Sphere, Heart & Hammock

The caged sphere for centre-of-room installs, the heart for weddings and romance work, and hammock or sling for tight ceilings.

05

Aerial Chains & Specialty Apparatus

Harder-edged pieces for darker rooms, available where a production has its own performer trained on them.

06

Rigging Hardware

Spansets, shackles, swivels and rated hardware supplied with inspection paperwork.

07

Costume Rental

Character costumes, stilt costumes, crowns, headpieces and performance wardrobe available to hire from the in-house wardrobe.

08

Wings & Large Wearables

Wings and oversized silhouettes for shoots, activations and events — the pieces that are expensive to build and used once.

09

Rig With an Operator

The rig plus Caroline as aerial coordinator: install, safety management and supervision for the run of the event — see Workshops & Coaching.

Where & How It Happens

Booking Rentals

Venues with no rigging

Ballrooms, warehouses, parking lots, open desert and festival fields — the whole reason the self-standing rig exists.

Photo & video shoots

Studios and location shoots that need a real apparatus for a day rather than a permanent install.

Producers with their own cast

Shows and festivals whose performers travel without apparatus.

Events & private parties

Costume and wing hire for themed nights, activations and content days.

Local Las Vegas only, for the rig

The rig and large apparatus are Las Vegas–based; travel outside the valley is quoted per project. Costumes can ship.

What the venue needs to provide

Figures tagged Caroline to confirm are drafted from standard industry practice and still need to be checked against her actual equipment before this page goes to a client.

Rig footprint
About 16 ft × 16 ft of level ground plus clearance, on a surface that will take the base plates Caroline to confirm
Rig setup
2–3 hours in, 1–2 hours out, with 1–2 crew for assist Caroline to confirm
Supervised use
The aerial rig is hired supervised — Caroline or an approved operator installs and oversees it. It is not dropped off unattended
Insurance
The hirer must carry their own liability cover for performers using the equipment; a certificate is required before release
Rates & duration
Quoted per day and per event; multi-day and run-of-show rates available Caroline to confirm
Deposit
A refundable damage deposit is held on all rentals Caroline to confirm
Costume sizing
State measurements when enquiring — rental wardrobe is built to Caroline's own sizing and not every piece will fit every body
Costume care
Returned cleaned and undamaged; alterations to rental pieces are not permitted
Lead time
Roughly 2–3 weeks for the rig; 1 week for costumes, subject to availability Caroline to confirm
