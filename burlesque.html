What the venue needs to provide

Figures tagged Caroline to confirm are drafted from standard industry practice and still need to be checked against her actual equipment before this page goes to a client.

Audience
18+ for the full version. State the audience and the venue's limits at the time of booking
Stage
A raised stage is strongly preferred so sightlines work past a seated front row
Lighting
A warm front wash and a follow spot or equivalent. Burlesque dies under flat white light
Music
Track supplied in advance; a house sound system with a 3.5 mm or DI input is needed
Green room
Private, lockable, with a mirror and a hanging rail — costumes are elaborate and quick-change is common
Set structure
Single numbers of 4–6 minutes, or a two-number set Caroline to confirm
Aerial burlesque
Adds every rigging requirement listed on the Aerial page
