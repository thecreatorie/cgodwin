What's in the Set

The Choreography & Act Creation Repertoire

01

Original Act Creation

A complete act built end to end for a single performer — concept, apparatus choice, music edit, choreography and costume design, delivered as a finished, performable piece.

02

Aerial Choreography

Sequencing for lyra, hammock, sling, chains, sphere, heart and the pentagram — built to the performer's actual strength and range, not to a template.

03

Fire & Flow Choreography

Hoop, fans, staff, poi and rope dart routines, including the transitions between props that most self-taught routines fall apart on.

04

Cast & Group Choreography

Duets, trios and full casts — staging, spacing, entrances and exits for multiple bodies in one space.

05

Show Direction & Running Order

For producers: shaping a bill so the night builds instead of flattening — running order, pacing, changeovers and how each act hands off to the next.

06

Music Editing & Cueing

Track edits, cut points and choreography written to specific hits — logo reveals, ceremony moments, drops.

07

Themed & Branded Numbers

An act written to a theme, a brand palette or a campaign concept rather than adapted from an existing routine.

08

Remote Act Design

Concept, apparatus selection, music edit and choreography notes delivered remotely, with video review rounds — no travel required.

Where & How It Happens

Booking Choreography & Act Creation

For individual performers

Working performers who need a signature act, or students moving from class material to something that is genuinely theirs. Often paired with private coaching.

For producers & shows

Revues, residencies, festival stages and one-off galas that need original content rather than a booking of existing acts.

For brands & agencies

A performance written around a product, a launch moment or a campaign idea.

For weddings & ceremonies

Choreography timed to a specific moment — a vow, a first dance, an exit — rather than a set dropped into the evening.

Remote

Act design, apparatus consultation and choreography can be run entirely over video.

Lead time
Roughly 6–10 weeks for a full original act; shorter for choreography on existing material Caroline to confirm
Process
Concept call, apparatus and music selection, choreography build, then review rounds before hand-off
Sessions
A full act is typically built across 6–10 sessions, in person or over video Caroline to confirm
Rehearsal space
Studio time with the correct apparatus and crash mats — supplied by the client or arranged in Las Vegas
Video review
Remote work needs clean, full-body video of every run for notes to be useful
Costume
Costume design and build can be included — see Costume Creation — and adds to the lead time Caroline to confirm
Rights
