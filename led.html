Where & How It Happens

Booking LED & Glow

Indoors, anywhere

No flame, no smoke, no permit. If the room can go dark, the act works.

Nightclubs & after-parties

Gogo sets, roaming LED, and short featured spots between DJ changeovers.

Corporate & brand activations

Programmed to brand colours; the most-requested option for launches, trade shows and product reveals.

Photo & content shoots

Long-exposure LED is built for cameras — the strongest option when the deliverable is footage rather than a live audience.

Fire-restricted venues

Where fire is refused, the same choreography runs on LED with almost no visible difference on camera.

What the venue needs to provide

Figures tagged Caroline to confirm are drafted from standard industry practice and still need to be checked against her actual equipment before this page goes to a client.

Lighting
The darker the better. House lights down or a dedicated dark corner — LED loses most of its impact under full white light
Performance area
Roughly 12 ft × 12 ft clear for hoops and staff; more for rope dart and poi Caroline to confirm
Overhead clearance
About 10 ft for staff and hoop work Caroline to confirm
Power
One standard 110 V outlet near the green room for charging between sets
Green room
Somewhere private to change and charge — LED costume changes are fiddly
Custom colours
Brand colour programming needs roughly 2 weeks' notice before the event Caroline to confirm
Set structure
Typically 3 × 15–20 minute roaming sets, or 2 × 5 minute featured sets Caroline to confirm
Haze
Not required, but haze or fog transforms LED and is strongly recommended where the venue allows it
