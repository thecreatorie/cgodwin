What's in the Set

The Workshops & Coaching Repertoire

01

Aerial Hoop / Lyra

Beginner through advanced. Conditioning, entries, shapes, spins, and sequencing into a routine.

02

Aerial Pentagram

Rare apparatus, rarely taught — she is one of the few people teaching it. Offered at festivals and privately.

03

Aerial Hammock & Sling

Wraps, drops and flow, for students who already have basic aerial conditioning.

04

Aerial Chains

A short, specialised class — chains behave differently from every other apparatus.

05

Aerial with Fire

Advanced only. Fire safety, prop handling, and how the two disciplines combine — taught at Southwest Flow Festival and Flowstorm.

06

Hoop & Flow Arts

Hula hoop, fans, staff and poi fundamentals for festival and community classes.

07

Flexibility & Conditioning

Splits, back flexibility and the strength work that everything else sits on.

08

Private Coaching

One-to-one in Las Vegas, including act creation — building a student their own routine, apparatus choice and costume.

09

Aerial Coordination

Caroline is the Aerial Coordinator for Southwest Flow Festival and Flowstorm. For festivals and producers: rig supply, safety management, scheduling and running the aerial programme end to end.

Where & How It Happens

Booking Workshops & Coaching

Festivals

Aerial hoop and hammock at Flowstorm, aerial hoop at Firedrums, single hoop at Southwest Flow Festival, plus Jungle Movement and similar — she travels with her own rig where the site has none.

Studios

She teaches weekly at Pole Fitness Studio in Las Vegas — aerial hoop, hammock, active flexibility and all-levels flow arts — and has taught aerial, flow and group choreography at Craft Motions. Guest workshops at aerial and pole studios elsewhere on request.

Private & small group

One-to-one and small groups in Las Vegas.

Corporate & team events

Introductory flow and hoop sessions as a group activity rather than a show.

Remote

Choreography, act design and costume consultation can be done remotely.

What the venue needs to provide

Class size
Roughly 8–12 students per aerial class for one instructor; larger for ground-based flow classes Caroline to confirm
Class length
Typically 90 minutes for a festival workshop; 60 minutes for a private Caroline to confirm
Rigging
Studio points, or her self-standing rig — she can teach at sites with no rigging at all
Mats
Crash mats under every point, supplied by the venue or arranged in advance
Student level
Please state the level when booking. Fire and pentagram classes have prerequisites
