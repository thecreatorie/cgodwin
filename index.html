The Story

About Caroline

Caroline Godwin is a Las Vegas circus performer, fire dancer, aerialist and burlesque artist, known for her signature Aerial Pentagram. She has performed at EDC, Formula 1, NASCAR, Burning Man and Las Vegas PRIDE, and is a sponsored performer for Juggling Calling and Pyrotechnique Las Vegas. She builds her own props, designs her own costumes, programs her own LED shows and teaches weekly at Pole Fitness Studio.
