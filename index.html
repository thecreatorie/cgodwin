The Story

About Caroline

Caroline began her journey in the circus arts with prop manipulation back in 2014, and now has over a decade of experience in flow arts. In 2018 she started her aerial journey and soon moved to Las Vegas to take her circus career to the next level. After training with world-renowned instructors, she became a professional circus artist in 2020.

Since then she has performed all over the country — at some of the largest events in Nevada including EDC, Formula 1, NASCAR, Burning Man, Las Vegas PRIDE, the Demolition of the Historic Tropicana, and the Grand Reopening of the Palms, plus Dreamforce, AWS, the Sin City Halloween Ball, and the Zouk, Terrace Afterhours and TAO nightclubs — sharing stages with artists like Marshmello, Tiësto, RÜFÜS DU SOL, Chris Lake, and Josh Teed.

Her most recognizable act is the Fire Aerial Pentagram, but the collection runs deep: Dragon Hoop, LED Clubs, Rhinestone Quad Hoops, LED Lyra in LED heels, stiltwalking with flow props or fire breathing, Fire Lyra with lycopodium — and the new Digital Fire concept, born in 2026. She designs and sews her own costumes, and her entertainment company can provide additional performers and equipment for any booking.

She is a sponsored performer for the fire performance companies Juggling Calling and Pyrotechnique Las Vegas, and she runs the Vegas Fire Dancers community on Instagram.
