The Story

About Caroline

Caroline Godwin is a Las Vegas circus performer, fire dancer, aerialist and burlesque artist, and the one behind the Aerial Pentagram. She has performed at EDC, Formula 1, NASCAR, Burningman and Las Vegas PRIDE. Her fire props are sponsored by Juggling Calling, with additional sponsorship from Pyrotechnique Las Vegas. She builds her own props, sews her own costumes, programs her own light shows and still teaches every week at Pole Fitness Studio. If you need someone who will bring it, this is her.
